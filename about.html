INTRODUCTION

Fitness is all about keeping our body healthy and active. It helps us build strength, improve endurance, and maintain a balanced lifestyle. Even simple exercises like push-ups, squats, and jogging can already make a big difference in our daily lives.

MOTIVATIONAL QUOTE

Success in fitness doesn't come from what you do occasionally, but from what you do consistently.

LIST OF
SIMPLE WORKOUTS

SQUATS – A lower body workout that targets your legs and glutes. Stand with feet apart, bend your knees like sitting on a chair, then stand up again.

PUSH-UPS – A bodyweight exercise that strengthens your chest, shoulders, and arms. Start in a plank position and lower your body, then push back up.

JUMPING JACKS – A cardio exercise that helps improve endurance. Jump while spreading your legs and raising your arms, then return to starting position.
